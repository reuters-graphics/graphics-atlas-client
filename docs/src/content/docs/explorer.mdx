Pick a country (or the world), slide the detail level, and toggle layers — **country polygons**, **country borders** and **disputed borders** — on and off. Inspect and copy the metadata, and download the TopoJSON shapes. Geometry is loaded by the client (from the CDN, or locally in dev).
